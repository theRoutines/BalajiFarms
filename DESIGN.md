## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the grounded provenance, absolute purity, and multi-generational heritage of authentic Indian artisanal agriculture. It blends an editorial culinary presence with direct-to-consumer clarity. The aesthetic balances warmth and rigorous purity: honoring time-tested Vedic craft—such as bilona-churned A2 cow ghee and slow wood-pressed oils—without succumbing to dated, rustic kitsch. 

Visual attributes draw heavily from raw terracotta pottery, unbleached cotton grain sacks, and dark loamy soil. The tone avoids cold industrial minimalism, instead embracing an organic, tactile luxury characterized by rich editorial typography, warm ivory planes, subtle deckled lines, and structured apothecary-inspired label blocks. Interactions should feel deliberate, generous, and transparent, reinforcing trust in lab certifications, origin tracking, and batch harvest numbers.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in unadulterated agrarian raw materials: sundried baked clay, lush foliage, golden cold-pressed oils, and rich tilled soil.

- **Primary (`#A64B32` - Terracotta):** The anchor of decisive actions and high-touch elements. Evokes hand-thrown clay curd pots and kiln-fired masonry. Reserved for primary conversion buttons, focused states, and key highlights.
- **Secondary (`#20432C` - Forest Green):** The stamp of botanical authenticity. Used for trust badges, farm-origin notes, organic certifications, lab results, and secondary brand accents.
- **Tertiary (`#D4B483` - Sand Gold):** The radiant essence of pure A2 bilona ghee and mustard flower meadows. Used for subtle background washes, decorative borders, award ribbons, and premium packaging indicators.
- **Neutral (`#382820` - Espresso Deep Earth):** A warm, rich brown-black replacing harsh synthetic blacks. Governs high-emphasis body text, headlines, and foundational contrast elements.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#F7F0E5` - Raw Ivory / Cloth Cream):** The warm background canvas across all layouts, eliminating sterile screen glare while evoking hand-pulled linen and milk cream.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for product display containers, elevated modal windows, and crisp checkout fields to highlight the rich amber and golden tones of the oils and ghee jars.
- **Border Neutral (`#E6DDD1` / `#D8CBBF` - Muted Husk):** Low-contrast warm borders that gently frame elements without mechanical stiffness.

## Typography

The type system juxtaposes the classical prestige of `Playfair Display` with the friendly, crystalline legibility of `Plus Jakarta Sans`. 

- **Editorial Serifs (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for product titles, brand affirmations, heritage storytelling, and section heroes. Italics in this serif are used sparingly for farm provenance details, harvest origins, and culinary tasting notes.
- **Modern Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Used across functional UI, technical batch reports, nutritional metrics, transactional carts, and checkout flows. Its rounded geometric construction guarantees high legibility across mobile screens without losing warmth.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows (`label-caps`):** Must always be styled in uppercase with intentional letter-spacing (`0.08em`) to mimic agricultural grain-sack stampings and vintage pharmaceutical laboratory seals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces generous breathing room, avoiding dense, discount-e-commerce clutter to highlight the artisanal, small-batch nature of the harvest.

- **Grid Architecture:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`> 1024px`) with `3rem` canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Tablet (`768px - 1023px`) transitions to an 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Mobile (`< 768px`) condenses to a 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Section Rhythm:** Narrative content alternates between wide, expansive storytelling blocks and tight, precise product shelves. Large vertical gaps (`space-2xl`) separate distinct narrative chapters (e.g., Vedic extraction process from customer reviews).
- **Product Display Density:** E-commerce product cards maintain balanced padding to allow product imagery—such as sunlit glass jars filled with golden liquid—to stand out uncluttered against warm ivory cards.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids harsh digital dropshadows or high-tech glass effects, employing instead a tactile physical layering strategy that mimics handmade rag paper, sun-dried clay labels, and natural ambient light:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Canvas:* Warm ivory (`#F7F0E5`).
  - *Primary Surface (Cards, Modules):* Pure milk white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale buff tint (`#FBF7F0`).
  - *Overlay/Floating:* Creamy solid white accompanied by diffuse, earth-tinted shadows.
- **Ambient Earth Shadows:** When elevation is necessary (e.g., sticky cart drawers, quick-add flyouts, elevated product hover states), use warm earth-tinted shadows:
  - *Resting card:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(56, 40, 32, 0.05), 0 0 0 1px #E6DDD1`
  - *Interactive hover:* `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(56, 40, 32, 0.08), 0 0 0 1px #D4B483`
  - *Modal & Drawer:* `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(56, 40, 32, 0.16)`
- **Craft Separation Borders:** Most depth is communicated not through vertical elevation, but through crisp, single-pixel warm borders (`#E6DDD1`) combined with tactile tonal shifts.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy combines architectural discipline with organic warmth:

- **Containers & Surfaces:** Medium roundedness (`0.5rem` / `rounded`) provides a soft, approachable frame that feels organic rather than rigid, applied consistently to cards, text inputs, and content wrappers.
- **Badges & Micro-Labels:** Full pill contours (`9999px`) are strictly enforced for tags (e.g., "Lab Certified", "Single Source", "Bilona Method", "Low Stock"), referencing medicine bottles and botanical farm tags.
- **Interactive Controls:** Buttons feature soft rounded corners (`0.5rem` to `0.75rem`), ensuring clear clickability without appearing playful or juvenile.
- **Image Treatments:** Product and farm lifestyle photography utilizes soft border radii (`0.75rem` to `1rem`) paired with subtle `1px` inner borders (`rgba(56, 40, 32, 0.06)`) to frame warm photography naturally against cream backdrops.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Terracotta background (`#A64B32`), pure white text, bold `label-lg`, `0.5rem` radius. Hover shifts to `#8C3B24` with a smooth 150ms transition. Padding: `0.875rem 1.75rem`.
- **Secondary Button:** Warm ivory canvas with a solid `1.5px` border in Espresso Deep Earth (`#382820`), matching dark text. Hover transitions to `#382820` with raw ivory text.
- **Tertiary / Botanical Action:** Forest Green (`#20432C`) fill with ivory text, reserved specifically for origin tracking, "Meet the Cows", and lab analysis reports.

### Badges & Chips
- **Purity Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.25rem 0.75rem`. 
  - *Certification:* Muted Forest Green background (`#EAF0EC`), Forest Green text (`#20432C`).
  - *Heritage/Process:* Sand Gold wash (`#F9F4EB`), dark brown text (`#382820`), bordered with `#D4B483`.
  - *Urgency/Stock:* Terracotta tint (`#F6ECE9`), Terracotta text (`#A64B32`).

### Product Cards
- Built on pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` border in `#E6DDD1` and `0.75rem` corner radius.
- Imagery framed against light neutral tone with gentle, warm lighting.
- Card contents include:
  1. Top pill badges indicating harvest method (e.g., "A2 Vedic Bilona").
  2. Headline serif for product name.
  3. Provenance subtitle ("Gir Cow Breed • Kutch, Gujarat").
  4. Volume/Weight selector toggle chips.
  5. Price display with unit cost breakdown.
  6. Quick Add terracotta action button.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Pure white background, `1.5px` border in `#D8CBBF`, `0.5rem` radius. Focus ring consists of a crisp `#A64B32` line with a soft `3px` terracotta halo at 15% opacity. Labels sit cleanly above in `label-md` Espresso.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted appearance using thick `1.5px` borders in `#382820`. Active state fills with `#A64B32` housing an ivory check icon.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Lab Batch Verification Card:** Architectural certificate-style module on soft Sand Gold canvas (`#FAF5ED`), featuring monospace batch codes, extraction date, moisture percentages, and direct downloadable lab PDFs.
- **Extraction Method Comparison Bar:** Dual-column comparative module detailing Wood-Pressed vs. Industrial Refined procedures with subtle divider rules and secondary green check marks.
